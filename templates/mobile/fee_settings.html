How Automation Works

  • Auto-generation: On the selected day each month, fees are created for all active students.
  • Extra Charges: Default extra charges are automatically added to every student's fee.
  • Due Date: Fee becomes due after the offset days from generation.
  • Late Fee: Fixed amount added per day for overdue fees.
  • Manual Generation: You can also generate fees instantly for the current month using the button above – all extra charges will be applied.
  • Start/Stop: Toggle automation on/off anytime.
