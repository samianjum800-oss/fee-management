How Automation Works

  • Auto-generation: On the selected day each month, the system creates fee records for all active students (with a defined fee structure or custom fee).
  • Extra Charges: The default extra charges are added to every student's fee when generated.
  • Due Date: Fee becomes due after the offset days from generation.
  • Late Fee: The fixed amount is added per day for overdue unpaid fees.
  • Start/Stop: Toggle automation on/off. When stopped, no automatic generation occurs.
