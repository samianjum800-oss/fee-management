# Dashboard and daily overview

## Overview

The AXIS dashboard is a starting point for the school administrator. It brings common school and fee activity together so you can spot work that may need attention. The mobile dashboard is a compact version and may show a notice when new fee records have been generated.

## Read the dashboard

1. Sign in to your school's administrator portal.
2. Open **Dashboard** from the left menu on desktop, or **Home** on mobile.
3. Review the summary figures and activity areas before starting the day's work.
4. Open a related page to investigate a number. For example, use **Fee Collection** to look into an outstanding balance, **Attendance** for today's class marks, or **Leave Management** for staff requests.
5. Return to the dashboard after recording work; recent totals may refresh after a short delay.

### Screen guide

```text
Desktop:  [School summary] [Fee summary] [Activity / alerts]
          [Collections or trends] [Classes / attendance / staff items]

Mobile:   [AXIS header]
          [Summary tiles]
          [Recent notice or activity]
          [Home | Students | Collect | Stock | More]
```

The exact tiles depend on available data and enabled features. The dashboard is an overview, not a replacement for the detailed Reports, Attendance, or Leave pages.

## Desktop and mobile

=== "Desktop"

    The desktop dashboard uses the school navigation menu. Choose a summary area or open its full module from the menu.

=== "Mobile"

    Tap **Home** in the bottom menu. If a new fee-generation notice appears, use its link to review the new voucher records or dismiss the notice after checking it.

!!! tip "Use the detailed page for decisions"
    Treat dashboard numbers as a quick signal. Open the underlying student, payment, attendance, or leave record before making a correction.

!!! note "New fee notice"
    The mobile dashboard tracks new fee-record IDs for the current browser session. A notice is not proof that a fee has been paid; check the student's fee record or the collection page.

## Common scenarios

- **Start of day:** Check attendance progress and pending staff leave requests, then open each detailed page.
- **After a fee run:** Review the fee-generation notice, then verify a sample student's voucher and due date.
- **A summary seems stale:** Open the source page and refresh. Some dashboard figures are cached briefly and may not update instantly.

## FAQ

### Why is a dashboard tile missing?
The feature may be disabled for the school, or there may be no records to show. Ask the school administrator or AXIS provider if a required feature is absent.

### Does a dashboard balance include every kind of charge?
Use the fee collection/student fee details to see the individual base fee, extra charges, late fee, amount paid, and remaining balance. The dashboard is not the detailed ledger.

### Can I mark attendance from the dashboard?
Use the Attendance page or the staff attendance page. The dashboard summarizes activity; the attendance register is the place to enter marks.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Try this |
|---|---|
| Totals do not change immediately after a payment | Refresh the dashboard and verify the receipt and fee record in Fee Collection or Reports. |
| Dashboard is blank | Check that you are in the correct school portal, that the relevant feature is enabled, and that records exist. |
| The mobile voucher notice remains visible | Dismiss it using the notice control or open the generated voucher link; the notice is informational. |
