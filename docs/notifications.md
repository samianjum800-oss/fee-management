# Notifications

## Overview

AXIS notifications surface important in-portal messages, including fee-generation activity and staff notices. School administrators see a notification bell in the school portal. Staff have a separate **Notifications** page in the staff portal. The two audiences do not share the same screen.

## School administrator: read a notice

1. Sign in to the school portal and choose the bell icon in the page header.
2. Review the unread badge and open **Notifications**.
3. Select a notice to follow its link when one is provided, or use the close/mark-read control to clear it from the unread list.
4. Choose **Mark all read** only after you have reviewed the listed messages.

The bell shows an unread count and the latest notices. Fee-generation notices may link to the relevant voucher list. On mobile, the bell is in the mobile header; a notice may also appear as a new-voucher banner on the dashboard.

## Staff: read a notice

1. Sign in to the staff portal.
2. Open **More** → **Notifications**.
3. Review the message and its date/time.
4. Follow up in the relevant area, such as Attendance or Leave, if the notice asks you to act.

The staff notifications page lists messages. If a notice is missing, refresh the page and check that **Staff Notifications** is enabled for your account/school.

```text
Administrator header: [School name]                 [Bell 3]
Notifications dropdown:
  New fees generated ... [time] [mark read]
  [Mark all read]

Staff portal: More > Notifications
  Message text
  Date and time
```

!!! tip "Open linked notices before clearing them"
    A notice may take you directly to vouchers or another relevant page. Follow the link and verify the information before marking everything read.

!!! note "In-portal messages"
    AXIS notifications appear inside the portal. They are not guaranteed email, SMS, or push notifications and should not replace your school's urgent communication process.

## Desktop and mobile

=== "School administrator"

    On desktop, choose the bell in the header to open its dropdown. On mobile, use the bell in the top bar. Select **Mark all read** to clear unread status after reviewing the list.

=== "Staff member"

    Use the staff bottom menu's **More** entry, then **Notifications**. Staff notifications are separate from the school administrator bell.

## Common scenarios

- **New fee records were generated:** Open the notice, go to Vouchers, and verify the month and sample student records.
- **A notice links to another page:** Open it, complete the relevant review, then mark it read.
- **You have many old unread notices:** Review them, then use **Mark all read**.

## FAQ

### Can I recover a notice after marking it read?
Read status changes the unread badge; it does not mean the notice was deleted. Refresh the list to review stored notifications.

### Why is there no notification bell on this page?
The bell appears in the school portal layout. Some screens, roles, or disabled features may not display notification controls.

### Do teachers see administrator notifications?
Staff use their own notification page. The administrator's tenant notification list is separate.

### Does the unread count mean I have a payment due?
Not necessarily. It counts unread notification messages. Open the notice to see what it concerns.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Badge does not update | Refresh the page, open the bell, and check the list. |
| Clicking a notice does not open its destination | The notice may not have a link. Check the message text and open the relevant module from the menu. |
| Staff Notifications menu is missing | Ask the administrator to confirm the staff-notifications feature is enabled. |
| A fee notice appears but no voucher is found | Open Vouchers and confirm the month; ask the administrator to check Fee Logs if the generation is incomplete. |
