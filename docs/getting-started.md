# Getting started with AXIS

## Overview

AXIS is your school's private portal for student records, fees, classes, attendance, staff, and related school work. The school administrator receives a school-specific sign-in link and administrator account from the person who set up the school's AXIS account. Teachers and other staff receive separate staff portal credentials from the school administrator.

There is no school self-sign-up screen in the portal. Your school data is separated from other schools. You normally stay in your school's portal; there is no school-switcher for administrators.

## Sign in as the school administrator

1. Open the AXIS link supplied by your school or AXIS provider. It includes your school's portal address.
2. Choose the school administrator login page if the link opens the public AXIS page.
3. Enter the administrator username and password given to you.
4. After sign-in, AXIS opens the first available page for your school. This may be the dashboard or another enabled feature.
5. Use the navigation menu to open the area you need. On a phone, use the bottom navigation and **More** for additional pages.

!!! warning "Use the right school link"
    Do not share a sign-in URL or password with another school. If you are sent to the wrong school portal or cannot tell which school you are in, stop before entering or changing records and ask your AXIS contact to confirm the link.

## Set up the school in a sensible order

1. Open **Settings** and add the official school name and logo. For a campus/wing school, create the main campuses and any sub-wings first.
2. Open **Classes & Subjects** or **Classes Management** and add the school's classes. Add subjects and class/subject assignments where needed.
3. Open **Staff Management** and create staff accounts. Give each person their own credentials and tell them to sign in through the staff portal.
4. Add students and assign each student to the correct class. For wing schools, select the correct campus/wing as well.
5. Set up monthly fees and due-date rules before generating the first month's fee records.
6. Configure the academic calendar, weekly holidays, annual holidays, and vacations before asking teachers to mark attendance or building timetables.
7. Assign timetable patterns to classes and teachers, then check the resulting class schedules.
8. Try one student, one test payment, one receipt, and one attendance mark before using AXIS for a full school day.

!!! tip "Start with a small check"
    Before a large import or a full month's fee generation, create a small number of records and check the class names, amounts, dates, and printed receipt. Correct the setup first if something looks wrong.

## Desktop and mobile

=== "Desktop"

    Use the left-hand menu to open **Dashboard**, **Fee Collection**, **Students**, **Staff Management**, **Classes & Subjects**, **Reports**, **Stock Management**, **Fee Structure**, **Vouchers**, **Fee Logs**, **Settings**, **Time-Table**, **Leave Management**, or **Attendance** when those features are enabled.

    The **Time-Table** section has separate pages for calendar setup, **Periods**, **Assign to Classes**, and **Assign to Teachers**.

=== "Phone or tablet"

    AXIS uses compact pages on mobile. The bottom menu provides **Home**, **Students**, **Collect**, **Stock**, and **More** when those modules are enabled. Open **More** for the remaining school tools.

    Mobile student, fee, stock, class, and staff pages are designed for smaller screens. Some administration tasks, especially detailed timetable editing and reporting, are easier on a desktop.

## Common situations

- **A teacher cannot sign in:** Ask the school administrator to verify that the teacher's account is active, the username/password are correct, and the staff portal is enabled. See [Staff portal](staff-portal.md).
- **A menu is missing:** The school may not have that feature enabled. Ask the school administrator or the AXIS provider; do not try random portal addresses.
- **You need to manage two schools:** Use the separate link and account for each school. AXIS keeps their records separate.
- **The school changed its name or logo:** Update it from [School settings](school-settings.md).

## FAQ

### Can I create my school's AXIS account from this page?
No. The code provides sign-in and school management pages, not a public school-registration workflow. Contact the AXIS provider or your school's designated administrator for account setup.

### Can one administrator switch between schools?
No school-switching control is present. Open the correct school-specific portal using the credentials for that school.

### Do teachers use the same login as the administrator?
No. Teachers use the staff portal and credentials created for their staff account. The school administrator uses the school's admin portal.

### Does AXIS work without an internet connection?
Some pages may show previously opened content, and student entries can be queued for later synchronization in supported browsers. Staff data and most actions need a working connection. Read [Mobile and installed apps](mobile-app.md) before relying on offline use.

## Troubleshooting

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| Login says the details are incorrect | Check for extra spaces, confirm the correct school link, and ask the account owner to verify/reset the credentials. |
| Login succeeds but a page redirects or is unavailable | Return to the home page and check which features are enabled for the school. Contact the AXIS provider if a required menu is missing. |
| The page looks incomplete on a phone | Refresh the page, check the network, or open the same page in a current browser. Use desktop for wide reports and timetable grids. |
| You are not sure which portal you opened | Sign out without editing records and confirm the school-specific link with your administrator. |
